## Findings

**Demand overall**
- In the AU Poll Gen Z sample, the top activities were ดูดวง/ลายมือ/ไพ่ยิปซี (77.4%), อธิษฐานขอพร (72.5%), worship at sacred sites (69.6%), lucky colours (60.3%) and lucky numbers (46.3%) [1].
- 62.0% of the same sample carry a sacred item, mostly พระเครื่อง or deity figures [3].
- 88% of Thais (aged 20–59) believe in mutelu. The top wish to attract is wealth (44%) [8].
- LINE service users are mostly aged 20–29 [5]. The DBD director-general says women make up nearly all buyers of muketing products, and that a typical item costs about 300–1,000 baht [7].
- No fetched source gives a gender split for any single deity or practice. Any claim that a specific figure is "popular with young women" is therefore [unverified].
- "73.2% of Gen Z are สายมู" appears in [9] with no named source, and the AU Poll page [1] does not contain it. Treat it as unsourced.

**Sacred figures**
- AU Poll: พระพิฆเนศ 76.8%. Also named, without per-item figures: ท้าวเวสสุวรรณ, เจ้าแม่กวนอิม, พญานาคราช, พระแม่ลักษมี. Monks: หลวงพ่อโสธร, หลวงพ่อทันใจ, หลวงพ่อโต and หลวงปู่ทวด together at 59.7% [2].
- Another AU Poll page names หลวงพ่อโสธร (the most-carried figure), หลวงพ่อทันใจ, หลวงพ่อโต, ท้าวเวสสุวรรณ, พระพิฆเนศ, พระพรหม and เจ้าแม่กวนอิม, plus the charms ปี่เซี๊ยะ and หินมงคล [3]. This confirms several existing picker items.
- LINE ดูดวง, H1 2025 [4]:
  - Ganesha is the most-requested figure.
  - พระแม่ลักษมี was the top love deity in 2024.
  - **พระราหู** prayers in May 2025 ran 5.5 times the 2024 monthly average.
  - **หลวงพ่อทันใจ** ranked #1 for แก้บน.
  - **ไอ้ไข่** (วัดเจดีย์) and หลวงพ่อโสธร are top vow sites.
- Thairath 2025 lists the deities behind the สายมู economy: พระพิฆเนศ, ท้าวเวสสุวรรณ, พระแม่ลักษมี, **พระพรหม**, เจ้าแม่กวนอิม, พระอาทิตย์ทรงรถ, พญานาค, หลวงพ่อทันใจ and ไอ้ไข่ [5].
- **พระตรีมูรติ** (CentralWorld) is presented as the love deity:
  - Visit on Thursday at 21:30, with 9 red incense sticks, 9 red roses and red candles [10][12].
  - Crowds peak around Valentine's Day, and most visitors are single [11].
  - In 2022 the campaign ran 10–24 Feb [11].
  - The statue is actually พระสทาศิวะ (five heads), but the public calls it ตรีมูรติ [11].
  - A Matichon headline reports crowds before Valentine's [17]. The detail that the ceremony falls on the Thursday before Valentine's is [unverified].
- **ท้าวมหาพรหม / พระพรหม (เอราวัณ)**:
  - Prayed to for work, study and exams, money, health and family, and luck.
  - Lakhon chatri vow dances happen almost all day.
  - The annual ceremony is on **9 Nov**, called "วันเกิดพระพรหม" (enshrined 9 Nov 2499) [13][17].
  - Fans pray here for concert tickets [14].
- Fan-mu for concert tickets is a young-audience use case [14]. The figures named are พระพรหม, พระพิฆเนศ (ห้วยขวาง), ท้าวเวสสุวรรณ, หลวงพ่อโสธร, พระธาตุพนม, หลวงพ่อทันใจ and ไอ้ไข่.
- ไท้ส่วยเอี้ย (แก้ปีชง) gets yearly how-to coverage. Thairath ran a 2569 edition, with worship starting at ตรุษจีน (17 Feb 2026) [15]. The source is a search snippet, so this is [unverified] in detail. No fetched demand numbers were found.
- เจ้าแม่ทับทิม (สะพานเหลือง) has only snippet-level evidence:
  - Open overnight at ตรุษจีน 2025.
  - A 2026 เทวสมภพ festival on 7–11 May.
  - Both are [unverified]. No demand data was found.
- No 2024–26 evidence was found for เทพทันใจ, ลูกเทพ, พระแม่กาลี, ท้าวจตุคามรามเทพ, พระสีวลี or พระสังกัจจายน์ [unverified / not found].
- Rahu crowd stories at วัดศีรษะทอง are from 2022–23, not recent. The ราหูย้าย date of 5 May 2025 is [unverified] (from a TrueID snippet).

**ของมู products**
- Product types named in the sources:
  - Phone wallpapers [5][6][8][16].
  - Jewellery: bracelets, necklaces, rings [6][7][9][16].
  - Clothes, bags and home textiles in birthday or lucky colours [6][8].
  - Cosmetics, perfume and lipstick with blessed or lucky ingredients [6][7][9].
  - Lucky phone numbers [6][8].
  - Deity debit cards [5][8].
  - Naga figures and ผ้ายันต์ [7].
- From [9], with no stated base: ดูดวง 33.9%, amulets/sacred objects 30.1%, lucky colours/numbers 26.9%.
- Market size claims vary and are loosely sourced: "more than 10 billion baht" [5] and "tens of billions" [7].
- DBD: 154 registered สายมู firms. 2023 revenue was 227.89M baht, up 52.92% [7].
- LINE ดูดวง: the top services are merit donations (coffin donation, ห่มผ้าพระธาตุ). ทำบุญสะเดาะเคราะห์ was up 121% month on month in March 2025 [4].
- No source gave sales numbers for bracelets, candles, phone cases, tarot decks or calendars.

## What shipped (2026-10-26)

The shortlist was applied with these changes:
- ids follow the repo's spelling: `luang_por_tan_jai` (not `luang_pho_tanjai`), `phra_phrom` (not `thao_maha_phrom`).
- พระราหู went to the protector group (tab ผู้คุ้มครอง, admin heading พญานาคและผู้ปกปักษ์) so the new items spread over four groups (Thai practice treats it as warding off bad luck at Buddhist temples); still open question 1.
- ไท้ส่วยเอี้ย was included: it is the only new item that matches a listed event today (the 2027 วันตรุษจีน practice note names it), and the monthly page already has a ปีชง feature.
- Step 3 took all three proposals (`scent_beauty`, `lucky_color_wear`, `lucky_number`), so step 3 now has 8 items.
- `ไอ้ไข่` was added as a second event-text needle for `ta_khai`; the label is ไอ้ไข่ since 2026-10-27 (owner decision; the id stays `ta_khai`).
- No date from this note was added to the event list: only verified events are listed, so หลวงพ่อทันใจ, พระพรหม, พระตรีมูรติ and พระราหู match no event today (they still count in the admin aggregate).

Spot check by the orchestrating agent (re-fetched 2026-10-26): AU Poll lucky colours 60.3% and lucky numbers 46.3% [1], LINE ดูดวง พระราหู 5.5x the 2024 monthly average, หลวงพ่อทันใจ "ครองอันดับหนึ่ง" for แก้บน, and ทำบุญสะเดาะเคราะห์ +121% month on month [4] are stated on the pages as cited.

## Open questions

1. Placement of พระราหู: does it belong in เทพฮินดู (a navagraha deity) or in พญานาคและผู้ปกปักษ์ (to balance group sizes)?
2. Depicting พระตรีมูรติ: the CentralWorld statue is พระสทาศิวะ (five heads) [11]. Should the stylized icon follow the statue, or show a generic three-faced form?
3. Should the existing ตาไข่ label become ไอ้ไข่? Every 2024–26 source writes ไอ้ไข่ [4][5][14].
4. Is ไท้ส่วยเอี้ย worth a slot, given that it is relevant mainly to people in a ปีชง year?
5. The gender skew per deity is unverified. Should we validate it with our own picker data after launch?
